Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Deutsch Lernen AI is a personal desktop tool used by its owner to analyze the owner's YouTube channel.

Data accessed

After Google OAuth authorization, it reads YouTube Analytics data, such as views, watch time and subscriber changes, and YouTube video metadata, such as titles, durations, publication dates and visibility settings.

Purpose

These data are used to prepare local performance reports for educational videos. The current application does not upload, edit or delete YouTube videos through the API.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens, reports and logs are stored locally on the owner's computer. This application does not intentionally sell or share YouTube data with advertisers. It communicates with Google APIs to retrieve authorized data. GitHub Pages may process standard website access data under GitHub's own policies.

Retention and deletion

The owner controls local files and may delete saved reports or tokens. Access can also be revoked via Google Account connections.

Future changes

If features change, such as automatic uploads or cloud-based AI processing of private analytics, this policy will be updated before they are enabled.

Contact

Questions may be raised through the project's GitHub issues. Do not post personal or sensitive information publicly.
